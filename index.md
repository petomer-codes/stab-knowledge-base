# STAB Knowledge Base

This is a knowledge base for the Space-Time Analysis Bayes (STAB) group, led by Dr. Jon Wakefield (University of Washington & University of British Columbia) and Dr. Zehang Richard Li (University of California, Santa Clara). Current group members can be found [here](https://sae4health.stat.uw.edu/team/STAB_group/). This was created with the intention of being a repository for the lab's background, resources, and code that has already been developed such that members can look back & new students can have an overview in one place. 

In the appendix, you can find the current group members, each with a list of what they are working on and have worked on in the past to foster collaborations. 

Use the sidebar to browse chapters, and reach out to Adi or Paige if you have a section you are interested in adding to the repo!
